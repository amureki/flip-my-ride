# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Three.js, TypeScript, Vite, chosen by the user. Static deployment to GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.
## Users
People opening a short, silly game shared on social media, including mobile users.
## Product Purpose
Park a sports car that moves only by flipping. Four progressively harder parking lots, drag-to-flip controls, instant retries. Next lot advances after parking; Retry restarts the current lot.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Fully client-side. No accounts or services. Short rounds and predictable movement. Inspired by the Roadster presentation.
## Product Principles
- Make the first flip immediate.
- Keep the target visible.
- Make failure funny and retry instant.
