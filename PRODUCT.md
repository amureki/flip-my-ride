# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web

## Stack

Three.js, TypeScript, and Vite. Static deployment to GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

People opening a short, silly game shared on social media, including mobile users.

## Product purpose

Park a sports car that moves only by flipping. Play seven increasingly difficult parking lots with drag-to-flip controls and instant retries. Next lot advances after parking; Retry restarts the current lot.

## Capabilities and constraints

The game runs in the browser without accounts or backend services. Rounds are short, and movement is predictable. The game is inspired by the Roadster presentation.

## Product principles

- Make the first flip immediate.
- Keep the target visible.
- Make failure funny and retry instant.
